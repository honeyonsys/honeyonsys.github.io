Frequently asked questions

Do you work with clients based in Europe from India?

Yes. I work remotely with European clients every week and keep my afternoon and evening hours aligned to CET/CEST, which gives a 5–6 hour daily overlap with teams in Germany, the Netherlands, the UK, the Nordics and the rest of the EU. Stand-ups, reviews and releases happen inside your working day, not after it.

What does a freelance web application developer cost?

Engagements are priced either hourly, as a monthly part-time retainer, or as a fixed-scope project with milestone payments. Rates depend on the stack, the level of architecture ownership and the length of the engagement. Send a short brief and you get an estimate with a suggested architecture and timeline, usually within two working days.

Which technologies do you build web applications with?

Java with Spring Boot, Python with Django and FastAPI, PHP with Laravel, Node.js with NestJS, Go, and C#/.NET on the backend; PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server and MongoDB for data; Redis and Elasticsearch for caching and search; Docker, Kubernetes, AWS and Azure for delivery.

Can you take over an existing codebase instead of starting from scratch?

Yes. A large share of my work is inherited code: legacy monoliths, unmaintained plugins, slow queries and half-finished migrations. Typical entry points are a code and architecture audit, a performance investigation, or a migration plan with a zero-downtime cutover.

How do you handle GDPR and data protection for EU clients?

I work under your DPA and sub-processor terms, keep personal data inside EU regions when your architecture requires it, and design for data minimisation, encryption at rest and in transit, audit logging, and deletion and export flows for data-subject requests.

How do contracts and invoicing work?

I sign your MSA, NDA and IP-assignment paperwork, or supply a simple contract if you prefer. European clients are invoiced in EUR or GBP by bank transfer or Wise; Indian clients are invoiced in INR with GST details. All source code and IP transfer to you on payment.

How quickly can you start a new project?

Small audits and fixed-scope pieces can usually start within a week. Longer engagements and full-time retainers typically start within two to four weeks depending on current commitments.

Do you also add AI features to existing web applications?

Yes. Common requests are retrieval-augmented search over company documents, AI copilots inside admin panels, ticket deflection for support teams, and document extraction for accounting and invoicing workflows, built on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or self-hosted models.
