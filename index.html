Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you take over and maintain our existing website?

Yes! Many of my clients already have a live website that just needs reliable maintenance. I can step in, audit the setup, keep all plugins and themes updated safely, fix existing bugs, and add any new features you need over time.

Do you work on both Shopify and WooCommerce?

Yes. I work extensively with both platforms. If you're on Shopify, I handle custom Liquid theme modifications, store setup, app setups, and checkout tweaks. If you're on WooCommerce, I handle custom plugin development, database speed optimization, theme customization, and server management.

Our store is slow or crashing during sales. Can you fix it?

Yes. Store slowdowns are usually caused by too many unoptimized plugins, heavy images, or database bottlenecks. I audit your website, clean up the clutter, optimize page loading, and ensure your checkout remains fast and error-free even during peak sales campaigns.

How does connecting our store to QuickBooks, Xero, or our ERP work?

I build automated sync systems so that whenever an order is placed on your website, it automatically creates the invoice and matches the payment in your accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, or Zoho). Stock levels and shipping details can also sync automatically so your inventory is always accurate.

Why work with an independent developer instead of an agency?

Agencies charge large monthly retainers to pay for account managers, sales reps, and office overhead — and your actual project is often handed down to inexperienced juniors. With me, you get direct access to a senior developer with 15+ years of experience. You get faster answers, higher quality work, and personal accountability.

How do we get started?

Simply reach out via email or phone / WhatsApp. Share your website URL and what you're looking to achieve (a new build, bug fixes, speed improvement, or ongoing maintenance). I will review your requirements and get back to you with a straightforward proposal and timeline within 24 hours.
